# Issues and Discussions

This repository is for public research communication. Contributions are welcome when they do not require access to private implementation or data.

## Issues

Use Issues for concrete, bounded items: documentation corrections, citations to consider, accessibility concerns in public material, or clearly scoped research questions. Include context and evidence where possible. Do not post secrets, personal data, private implementation details, or confidential reports.

## Discussions

Use Discussions for open-ended feedback on the thesis, research questions, conceptual interaction patterns, and public articles. Say whether a point is a personal observation, hypothesis, or study evidence.

## Maintainer expectations

Responses and issue closure are not guaranteed. Maintainers may edit labels and close items outside the public scope. Contributions do not grant access to private plans or implementation. Do not submit code or datasets unless a contribution guide explicitly invites them.

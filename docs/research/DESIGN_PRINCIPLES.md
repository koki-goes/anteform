# Design principles

These principles guide exploration. They are not claims about completed implementation.

1. **Keep human judgment visible.** Make clear where the person must decide and what the system has prepared.
2. **Make context inspectable.** Show where information came from and signal when it may be incomplete, stale, or uncertain.
3. **Expose gaps.** Treat missing context as a first-class state instead of silently inventing an answer.
4. **Support authored outcomes.** Help people turn understanding into a deliberate output while preserving their control over consequential content and actions.
5. **Reduce interaction overhead.** Aim to make relevant work easier to recognize without repeated prompting.
6. **Minimize data.** Use only the information needed for the task and avoid exposing private data in public artifacts.
7. **Earn claims with evidence.** Distinguish concepts, prototypes, observations, and validated results.

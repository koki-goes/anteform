# Research questions

These are open questions; listing them does not imply they have been answered.

1. **Recognition:** What helps people notice relevant work without creating noise or distraction?
2. **Context:** Which information is necessary for sound judgment, and how can its source, freshness, and uncertainty be inspected?
3. **Missing information:** How can a system expose gaps and support finding information without filling them with unsupported guesses?
4. **Human control:** Which actions can be prepared or automated safely, and which decisions or outputs should require explicit human authorship?
5. **Interaction:** When does a dedicated work surface help more than chat or an existing application?
6. **Evaluation:** What study designs and measures can test comprehension, decision quality, effort, trust calibration, and user control without overstating results?
7. **Privacy:** How can useful context be provided while minimizing collection, retention, and exposure of personal data?

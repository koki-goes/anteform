# Beyond Chat

*Manifesto of Anteform — an AI-Native Execution Surface*

> **Draft for public discussion.** Based on the project note *実行基盤について(2).pages* and the conversation summary. The edited WritingBlock referenced in the conversation was not available in the provided preview; this draft is not represented as that exact manuscript.

## AI should make work easier to judge, not take judgment away

AI applications are often built because they are possible, not because they answer a real need. A more useful starting point is to ask what people want to accomplish in everyday work.

Many tasks involve more than execution. A person must recognize what matters, understand its context, decide what to do, and shape an outcome they can stand behind. Delegating the entire chain to an agent may remove the person from the decisions and creations that make the work meaningful.

We are exploring a different kind of software: an **AI-native execution surface for human work**. It prepares the context, information, and interfaces that help a person make a decision and produce an outcome. It may gather relevant material, expose missing information, and make possible next actions easier to inspect. The person remains the author of consequential judgment and output.

The experience should make work easier to see and understand without demanding constant prompting. It should help people find the right information in the right place, with enough context to judge whether it is relevant. When information is missing, the system should help reveal that gap and support deliberate inquiry rather than conceal uncertainty.

This is a research direction, not a claim that the problem is solved. We will investigate what context is useful, how it should be presented, which decisions should remain with people, and how to evaluate whether the interface helps. We will share methods and evidence when they can be disclosed safely.

# Public disclosure policy

This repository is a selective public research record, not the source of truth for the private implementation.

## Suitable for public release

- Problem framing, manifesto, and high-level design principles.
- Research questions and non-sensitive study methods.
- Experiments using synthetic, public, or explicitly consented data, after privacy and disclosure review.
- Results with enough method, scope, limitations, and artifacts for interpretation.
- Technical writing that does not reveal protected implementation.

## Keep private

- Source code or internal algorithms designated confidential.
- API keys, tokens, credentials, private endpoints, and security-sensitive operational details.
- Personal, customer, employer, or confidential third-party data and identifiable traces.
- Detailed internal roadmaps, unreleased product plans, and private research notes.
- Any artifact whose rights, consent, or disclosure status is unclear.

## Before publishing

1. Check secrets, personal data, third-party rights, and confidential details.
2. Confirm examples and datasets are synthetic, public, or covered by explicit permission.
3. Label the evidence level: concept, prototype, exploratory observation, or evaluated result.
4. Include method, scope, limitations, and date for empirical claims.
5. Review repository history and generated artifacts, not only the visible document.

If sensitive information is exposed, remove or rotate affected secrets as appropriate and follow the platform's history-removal process. Deleting a visible file alone may not remove prior copies.

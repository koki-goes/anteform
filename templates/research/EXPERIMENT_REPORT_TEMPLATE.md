# Experiment report: {question}

- Status: protocol / running / analyzed
- Date and version:
- Evidence level: exploratory unless otherwise justified

## Hypothesis

State a falsifiable hypothesis and what result would count against it.

## Protocol

Describe task, conditions, measures, inclusion criteria, and analysis plan before results.

## Data and consent

Describe data source and consent. Link only approved, de-identified or synthetic public artifacts.

## Results

Report the observed results with appropriate uncertainty. Avoid causal or performance claims the design cannot support.

## Limitations

Sample, confounds, measurement limits, and reproducibility constraints.

## Reproduction materials

Link approved materials or explain why they cannot be shared.

## Conclusion

State what the evidence supports, what it does not, and the next question.

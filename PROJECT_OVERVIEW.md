# Anteform — project overview

**Status:** early research and development; no public product release.

## Thesis

Explore an AI-native execution surface that prepares relevant context and interfaces so people can recognize work, make informed judgments, and create outputs. Automation may assist, while human understanding and authorship remain central.

## Problem framing

Chat-based interaction can require people to repeatedly formulate requests and reconstruct context. Fully delegated agents can obscure how an outcome was reached or move consequential judgment away from the person doing the work. This project investigates whether a context-aware work surface can reduce that friction while keeping decisions legible and user-directed. This is a hypothesis, not a demonstrated finding.

## Areas of exploration

- How people notice and understand work that needs attention.
- How relevant context can be found and presented with provenance and uncertainty.
- How a person can move from understanding to deliberate action and an authored outcome.
- How to evaluate usefulness, trust, control, and cognitive burden.

## Public repository boundary

Private implementation, internal algorithms, credentials, personal or customer data, detailed product plans, and operational security information are out of scope.

## Current evidence

The project note describes a conceptual progression: open a tool, recognize work, begin, discover missing information, find and integrate it, form an output, and complete the task. This is a framing device, not a measured workflow or shipped feature.
